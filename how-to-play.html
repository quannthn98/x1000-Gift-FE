📋 Game Rules

  • Betting: Both players must bet the same amount. Minimum bet varies by room.
  • Fairness: Every game uses cryptographic proof to ensure fairness. You can verify any result.
  • Platform Fee: 2% fee on winnings. No fee if you lose.
  • Timeouts: If a player doesn't join within 5 minutes, the game is cancelled and bets are refunded.
  • Fair Play: Cheating, collusion, or any unfair practices will result in account suspension.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gift_x1000 provably fair? +
Yes! Every game uses cryptographic hashing to ensure complete fairness. You can verify any game result using our fairness proof system. The outcome is determined by a combination of server seed, client seed, and nonce, making it impossible to manipulate.
What are the fees? +
We charge a 2% platform fee on winnings only. If you lose, there's no fee. For deposits and withdrawals, network fees apply (typically 0.5-1 TON).
How long do withdrawals take? +
Withdrawals are typically processed within 5-15 minutes. During high traffic, it may take up to 1 hour. You'll receive a notification when your withdrawal is complete.
Can I cancel a game I created? +
Yes, you can cancel a game as long as no opponent has joined yet. Once an opponent joins, the game must be completed.
What happens if there's a connection error? +
If you disconnect during a game, the game will continue automatically based on your chosen side. The result will be saved and you can view it in your game history. Your funds are always safe.
Is there a maximum bet? +
There's no hard maximum, but individual rooms may have their own limits. You can also set personal betting limits in Settings under Responsible Gaming.
How do I verify game fairness? +
Click the "View Fairness Proof" link on any completed game. You'll see the server seed, client seed, and nonce used to generate the result. You can independently verify this using any SHA-256 calculator.
